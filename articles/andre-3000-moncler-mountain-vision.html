André 3000 Brings Unreleased Music and Visual Art to Moncler

The OutKast innovator contributes an unreleased score and a hand-drawn mountain scene to Moncler’s Re/Icons campaign.

André 3000 has added another unexpected chapter to his post-OutKast creative life. For Moncler’s Re/Icons campaign, the Atlanta artist supplied an unreleased piece of music for a short film built around the brand’s mountain-inspired archive.

The collaboration is more than a soundtrack placement. During the shoot, André drew a mountain landscape in white chalk across a black studio backdrop. That image became part of the visual direction, giving the campaign a handmade element that reflects his interest in moving between music, design and visual art.

The project revisits four pieces from Moncler’s archive: the Karakorum, Verone, Himalaya and Tibb. The 2026 collection expands beyond outerwear into a broader ready-to-wear range.

Moncler has not announced a title or standalone release for André’s new music. For now, the unreleased piece lives within the campaign film rather than signaling a new single or album rollout.

It is another example of André following his own creative instincts. Since New Blue Sun and the piano-centered 7 piano sketches, he has continued to explore sound and image without being bound to the expectations of a conventional rap release.
