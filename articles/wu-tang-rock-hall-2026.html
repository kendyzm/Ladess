Wu-Tang Clan Takes Its Place in Rock History

The Staten Island collective marked its Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction with a celebration of the catalog that helped reshape rap.

More than three decades after introducing the world to Enter the Wu-Tang (36 Chambers), Wu-Tang Clan has reached another milestone: induction into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame.

The 2026 ceremony brought together key members of the collective, including RZA, GZA, Method Man, Raekwon, Ghostface Killah, Inspectah Deck, U-God, Masta Killa and Cappadonna. The group accepted the honor from comedian Chris Rock before performing “C.R.E.A.M. (Cash Rules Everything Around Me)” and “Shimmy Shimmy Ya.”

The moment carried extra meaning for a crew that built its reputation outside the traditional industry playbook. Wu-Tang’s raw production, martial-arts references, rotating solo careers and unmistakable New York identity helped establish a blueprint that generations of artists have studied.

Method Man used the acceptance moment to address the skeptics who once doubted the group’s place in the mainstream. Wu-Tang becomes the 16th hip-hop act inducted into the Hall.

The ceremony was not livestreamed. A televised broadcast is scheduled for December 8 on ABC and Disney+.
